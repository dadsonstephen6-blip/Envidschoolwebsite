HOW ADMISSION WORKS

Our Admission Process

The admission process generally follows these simple steps.

01

Contact the School

Parents or guardians can contact the school to enquire about available admission opportunities.

02

Get Admission Information

The school provides information about the appropriate class, requirements, fees and other important details.

03

Visit the School

Parents and guardians may visit the school to learn more about the learning environment and facilities.

04

Assessment or Interview

Where applicable, the student may take part in an assessment or interview as part of the admission process.

05

Admission Decision

The school reviews the student's information and communicates the admission decision.

06

Complete Enrollment

Once admission is confirmed, parents complete the required enrollment procedures with the school.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Speak With the School for Current Details
