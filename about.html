OUR STORY

A Place Where Every Child Can Grow

Envid School is a learning community dedicated to providing quality and holistic education for children and young people. We believe that every child is unique and observes an educational environment where they can learn, grow, and thrive.

Our school provides a a balanced education that develops students academically, socially, emotionally, and morally. Through dedicated teachers, engaging lessons and a supportive school environment, we prepare our students for success in further education and in life.

At Envid school, we don't only teach childdren how to learn, think independently, work with others and become responsible members of the society.
